Nitro: Time, Expenses, Mileage · Account and personal cloud-data requests

Request deletion without the app

Email support@nitrotrack.app from the email address associated with your Nitro account. Use the subject “Delete my Nitro account” and tell us you want your account and associated personal cloud data deleted.

Email a deletion request

You do not need to reinstall Nitro or buy a subscription to make this request. We verify account ownership before acting. If you cannot access your account email, contact support for help. Never send your password or a verification code in your deletion email.

Request deletion in Nitro

Sign in to the account you want to delete, open Settings and your account, then choose Account & recovery and the account-deletion option. Review the confirmation and recovery deadline shown in the app. A workplace owner must transfer ownership or close the workplace before deleting their login.

Signing out, uninstalling Nitro, or choosing to purge and disconnect a device is not the same as requesting account deletion.

Recovery and removal

Once an eligible deletion request has been verified and scheduled, it normally has a 14-day recovery window. To recover, sign in to the original account and explicitly cancel deletion before the displayed deadline. Creating another account does not cancel the original request. Contact support if you need help with a support-requested deletion.

After that window, the login and eligible personal cloud records are scheduled for permanent removal. These include associated personal sessions, mileage, expenses, receipt files, and personal settings. Removal is processed by the service; it is not a promise that all copies disappear at the exact deadline.

Download any records and original receipt files you want to keep before proceeding. Files already exported or shared, and copies remaining on your devices or with recipients, are separate from Nitro’s cloud records.

Workplace records and retained information

Deleting your login or removing a workplace membership does not automatically delete your employer’s records. Workplace payroll history, approved payroll snapshots, audit history, deletion markers, security records, and backups may be retained separately under preservation or recordkeeping holds. Unrelated accounts and other workplaces are not deleted with your request.

Some workplace closures or cloud-access expirations have a server-displayed 120-day archive/export window. This is not a guarantee that all employer records are erased after 120 days. Protected or legacy workplaces may need operator-assisted handling. Contact support and your workplace administrator about the records retained for your account and the applicable deadline.

For details of the information Nitro handles and its retention practices, read the Nitro privacy policy.
