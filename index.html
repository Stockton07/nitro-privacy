Privacy Policy

Nitro: Time, Expenses, Mileage

Effective October 18, 2026

Nitro is local-first. You can use its core time, mileage, expense, and payroll tools without signing in. Cloud sync is optional and begins only after you choose to connect an account.

Who provides Nitro

Nitro is a software product provided by Stockton Norris, an individual operating in Utah, United States. Nitro is not a separate company or a registered LLC. The initial service is intended for users in the United States. Contact: support@nitrotrack.app.

Information Nitro handles

Without a cloud account, Nitro saves your records on your device rather than uploading them to Nitro’s cloud service. A paired Apple Watch exchanges supported records and preferences with your iPhone. You may also choose to export or share records using your device’s sharing tools.

Signing in sends the account information needed to authenticate you, even before you connect record sync. It does not by itself upload your on-device work records. Signed-in apps also report limited operational information: the app and build version, operating-system major version, and a randomly generated installation identifier associated with your account. This helps Nitro support compatible versions. It is normally reported once a day or after an app update, not through continuous device tracking. It does not include a hardware serial number, device name, advertising identifier, location, work records, or password.

If you enable cloud sync, Nitro sends the following information to its cloud service so your records can remain available across your signed-in devices:

  • Account information, including your email address, display name, and account identifier.
  • Work sessions, including clock-in and clock-out times, pay rate, work category, time zone, and notes you enter.
  • Mileage records, including dates, distance, reimbursement rates and amounts, purpose, and notes.
  • Expense records, including dates, amounts, categories, descriptions, notes, and any receipt files you attach.
  • Shared work preferences, including your expected workday, hourly pay rate, and mileage rate.
  • Payroll schedules, including period boundaries, payday timing and weekend handling; submitted or locked period status; and credited time-gap classifications.
  • Workplace account information, where applicable, including organization name, account association, membership role, and membership status.
  • Cloud-access records, including the features available to an account or organization, license or complimentary-access status, effective dates, and administrative changes to these records.
  • Workplace review records, including decisions, correction reasons, before-and-after changes, review requests, payroll approval/submission/payment-status snapshots, and activity read or acknowledgement status.
  • Workplace invitations, including the invited email address, assigned role, expiry, acceptance status, and a single-use invitation code.

Siri and delivery choices, appearance settings, and payroll recipients remain on your device. Workplace payroll download preferences, including whether to include disabled employees and gross wages, are stored with workplace settings so authorized administrators see consistent defaults. A server-managed planning profile may enable an estimated net-pay calculation for selected accounts using supplied approximate age, city/state, and filing status. Other accounts show gross wages and reimbursements. Estimates are not payslips or tax advice; Nitro does not look up your personal tax details on the internet.

How information is used

Nitro uses this information to provide app functionality: authenticating accounts, saving and syncing records, resolving sync conflicts, preparing payroll summaries, maintaining authorized cloud access, restoring information on another signed-in device, and providing support. Administrative audit records help secure the service and document changes to workplace membership and access rights.

Cloud service

Nitro uses Supabase to provide account authentication, database storage, file storage, and live synchronization. Cloud records are associated with your account so that only authorized access can retrieve them. Supabase processes information on Nitro’s behalf under its own privacy policy.

Authentication emails are delivered through Resend. These include verification and password-recovery codes. Your email address and the message content are processed for delivery and security; Nitro does not use these messages for advertising. Codes expire, are checked by the server, and should never be shared. Read Resend’s privacy policy for its practices.

Workplace accounts and administrator access

An approved workplace can associate existing accounts with its organization and assign membership roles. Organization and account access may be provided under a workplace license or complimentary access. A complimentary access record is not a payment and does not contain payment-card information.

Authorized workplace administrators may review their organization’s cloud-synced timesheets, expenses, mileage, credited work, and attached receipts. They can approve entries, return them for correction, deny reimbursements, or correct supported fields with a recorded reason. The service preserves the original and revised values in review history. Workplace access does not grant access to another organization’s records or unrelated personal accounts. Privileged service operators may access records for support, security, and service operation.

Workplaces control payroll schedules, future-session pay rates, mileage defaults, payroll-sharing availability, and whether employees submit payroll or management handles it. Existing records retain their recorded rates. Locked payroll periods restrict record changes across connected devices, and administrative policy/access changes are recorded in a restricted audit log.

Administrators can configure guarded automatic approval for new entries, manage workplace seats and invitations, and mark payroll as approved, submitted, or paid. These payroll labels record the organization’s status; Nitro does not transfer wages or independently verify that payment occurred. Approved payroll snapshots and later changes remain in history.

Employees can view workplace decisions and payroll updates in an in-app activity inbox with an unread badge. Read and acknowledgement status are stored with the activity. Nitro does not send workplace push notifications or system alerts. When all entry categories are configured for automatic approval, the employee inbox is hidden; administrators retain audit history.

Your organization may have its own employment-record policies and obligations. Ask your administrator how it uses and retains your work information.

Ending membership or access does not automatically delete work records. Contact your workplace administrator or Nitro support about correcting records, access changes, or deletion requests.

Support and browser tools

When you contact support, we process the name, email address, platform/version, topic and message you submit to respond and investigate. Workplace owners and administrators can create support tickets, optionally associate an employee in their own organization, and view status and messages. An employee association does not automatically attach that employee’s payroll, work records or receipts. Authorized workplace administrators and Nitro support operators can read the ticket conversation.

Support tickets and messages are stored with the service; Resend may deliver supplementary notifications containing support-message content and a ticket reference. Do not include passwords, email codes, payment-card details or unnecessary private employee information. Support and security records may be retained when needed to resolve requests, prevent abuse or meet applicable obligations; account deletion does not necessarily delete an employer’s support history. Contact support about correction or deletion.

When browser tracking is offered, browser pages request the records needed for the open view. Confirmed work history is not intentionally maintained as a permanent full browser replica. Unsent edits and selected pending receipt files can remain in that browser until synchronized or explicitly removed. Clearing site data can discard unsent changes; signing out does not itself erase them. Browser sign-in uses a protected session cookie; appearance preferences may be stored locally. Public support forms use Cloudflare Turnstile and limited request information to prevent abuse.

Licenses and payments

Nitro maintains account and organization access records, including complimentary beta and lifetime access. A personal access code applies only to personal cloud access, not workplace membership or administrator permissions. Personal signup and password recovery use server-verified email codes. Beta availability is controlled by the server, including for older app versions.

The launch offer, when enrollment opens, provides three calendar months of personal cloud sync or Workplace access for up to 30 total active people, including owners and administrators. Eligible signup must begin before February 1, 2027 at midnight Mountain Time. Signup displays the exact server-confirmed expiration. We keep offer version, acceptance time and start/end dates to administer access. Future pricing has not been determined. No card or automatic payment is created by this offer. Continuing cloud service afterward requires a separate voluntary paid subscription if offered; local-only use remains available, subject to workplace archive and record protections. Existing personal beta and lifetime grants follow their own terms. See the launch offer terms.

Where offered, workplace subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Nitro keeps the organization’s Stripe customer, checkout, subscription and invoice-event references, seat quantity, plan interval, payment/access status, paid-through date, scheduled changes and cancellation status. This is used to verify access and avoid duplicate billing. Nitro does not receive full payment-card numbers or card security codes; payment details are entered on Stripe-hosted pages. See Stripe’s privacy policy.

Only an authenticated workplace owner can request billing changes. Payment-method updates and invoices use a short-lived authenticated Stripe portal. Seat and renewal changes are confirmed in Nitro and checked against assigned members and reserved invitations. Cancelling stops renewal and does not remove access already paid for before its end date. Complimentary access does not require a paid subscription while the grant remains valid.

Personal app-store purchasing is separate and is available only when its products and server verification are enabled. Purchases made through an app store are managed with that store. Purchase options can differ by distribution channel and storefront. A successful browser return or an on-device payment result does not by itself grant cloud access.

Photos, files, and contacts

Nitro accesses the camera or photo picker only when you choose to attach a receipt. Attached receipt files sync only when cloud sync is connected.

Nitro’s contact picker lets you select a payroll recipient. Nitro does not upload your address book or contact selection to its cloud service. Apple’s Messages and Mail interfaces handle the delivery you approve.

No tracking or advertising

Nitro does not contain advertising, sell personal information, use advertising identifiers, or track your activity across other companies’ apps or websites. Nitro does not use third-party analytics SDKs.

Security

Nitro uses encrypted network connections, system-protected credential storage, database access rules, and protected on-device storage. Workplace membership requires authorized administrator or service action; accepting an administrator’s invitation also requires a matching verified email, valid invitation, and available licensed seat. An ordinary account cannot grant itself administrator privileges, change paid entitlements, or award complimentary access. Authorized administrators and service operators may have access as described above. No method of electronic storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

Your choices and deletion

You may use Nitro without cloud sync, disconnect a device, sign out, or purge this device’s local records. Purging and disconnecting also signs you out; it is not a request to erase the workplace’s cloud records. Deleting a supported record while sync is connected requests its corresponding cloud deletion, subject to workplace review, payroll locks, and retention protections.

Account deletion is available through Account & recovery where enabled, or through support. A deletion request normally has a 14-day recovery window. To recover, sign in to the original account and explicitly cancel deletion before the displayed deadline. Creating a different account does not cancel the original request. After the window, the login and eligible personal records are scheduled for permanent removal. Owners must transfer ownership or close their workplace first.

Removing a workplace member is separate from deleting that person’s global login. Authorized administrators can restore a removed member during the displayed 14-day recovery window, subject to an available seat. Removing membership does not delete unrelated personal accounts or another workplace’s records.

A known cloud-license expiry or workplace closure can start a 120-day archive/export window. Administrators should download the full record archive and original receipt files before the server-displayed deadline. Employer records, approved payroll snapshots, audit history, deletion markers, security records and backups may be retained separately where preservation or recordkeeping holds apply; a login-deletion request does not automatically erase these employer records. Some legacy or protected workplaces require operator-assisted retention handling. Contact support about the exact retention and available recovery for your account.

Operational installation/version entries are removed after 120 days without a report, in bounded cleanup batches, and when their associated login is permanently removed. Nitro does not use them to grant access or authorize a device. Signing out or ending a license is not an account-deletion request.

Children’s privacy

Nitro is a work and payroll utility and is not directed to children under 13. Nitro does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
